# Experiments excluded from the default public reproduction

The original repository is preserved. The exclusions below refer to analyses described by the paper that need files or annotations beyond the released model-training package; they are not silently substituted with unrelated data.

| Script/module or paper analysis | Experiment | Required data | Availability | Public equivalent | Partial reproduction | Exclusion reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `projects/placenta/pathologist_analysis/analysis.py` and paper infarction analysis | Compare healthy placentas with infarction; clinical subgroup and slide-level cell/tissue statistics | In-house clinical WSI cohort, diagnosis/outcome metadata, slide-level predictions | Paper states remaining in-house placenta slides and clinical data are unavailable under ethics approvals/data transfer agreements | No | Public model training/evaluation remains possible; not the cohort comparison | Excluded from default workflow; no fabricated diagnoses or replacement cohort |
| Paper healthy-term quantification (30 WSIs) | Aggregate learned predictions across healthy placenta slides | Healthy-term WSI cohort and patient/sample metadata | Not in the public Zenodo training archives; paper data statement restricts remaining in-house slides | No | Train models and evaluate released held-out tile/graph splits | No public full slide-level cohort equivalent |
| Paper pilot infarction study (12 WSIs from 8 placentas) | Compare infarcted and healthy cases | Restricted WSIs and clinical metadata | Not publicly released under governing ethics/data transfer arrangements | No | None beyond model training | Clinical cohort inaccessible |
| Supplementary Fig. 1 cross-organ/membrane/cord generalisation | Transfer evaluation across GTEx and other tissue WSIs | Separate GTEx WSIs plus membrane-roll, cord and second-trimester placenta slides | These data are not included with the HAPPY training data; no validated access path is packaged with this project | No exact equivalent | The published placenta test sets can still be evaluated | Not an input to the three primary reported metrics; excluded pending valid public source and compatible labels |
| Four-pathologist tissue validation | Inter-pathologist agreement and model-vs-expert comparison on 180 structures | Expert labels and associated validation task data | Protocol appears in Supplementary Methods; labels are not in the HAPPY public model-training archives | No | GNN can be evaluated against the released author annotations | Human-validation outputs cannot be reconstructed from author ground truth alone |

The two graph source slides at BioImage Archive S-BIAD1045 are public CC BY 4.0 and are not excluded. The public graph embeddings and annotations permit the original graph model experiment without processing those WSIs from scratch; raw-slide upstream inference remains a separate, optional reconstruction path.
